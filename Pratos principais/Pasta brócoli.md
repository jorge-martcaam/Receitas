## Ingredientes 
400g plain flour
280-300g broccoli florets
1 Tbsp salt

## Instruccións
1) Add 1 cup of water and the salt to a saucepan and bring to a boil. Add broccoli and simmer for 5-8 minutes until fork tender. Drain well and allow to cool slightly.
2) Add the cooked broccoli and flour to a food processor and blend until a dough forms. Knead for 5 minutes, then cover and rest for 20 minutes.
3) Divide dough in half. On a floured surface, roll one piece into a thin rectangle, flour the dough and fold it over itself 3 times. Dust with flour, then slice into noodles and toss through more flour (or rice flour) to prevent sticking. Repeat with remaining dough.
4) Cook noodles in a pot of salted boiling water for 4-5 minutes, or until cooked to your liking. Drain and toss through your favourite sauce. DEVOUR!
