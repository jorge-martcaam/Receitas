## Instruccións
Especias de dentro a fora.

If you have made seitan, you know it is chewy. 

But have you ever added beans? 

It gets in the way of gluten and makes the seitan more tender. 

It is perfect for meats like sausages, chicken tenders and deli slices.

What I like about deli slices is that you can add any seasoning you like and have a different sandwich every week.

Make sure to steam it well until cooked, store in the fridge to make perfectly thin slices. 

One loaf, a week of sandwiches. You never want store-bought again.
