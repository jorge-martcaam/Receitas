## Ingredientes

Dry ingredients (mix in a large bowl):

	●	1½ cups vital wheat gluten (essential for the texture—cannot be substituted)
	●	¼ cup nutritional yeast (adds a rich umami flavor)
	●	1 tsp granulated garlic
	●	2 tsp smoked paprika (for that grilled color and flavor)
	●	A little salt and black pepper
	●	A pinch of nutmeg (the secret to that classic hot dog flavor)

Wet ingredients (blend until completely smooth):

	●	1 cup cooked white beans (canned or home-cooked—they make the sausages soft and juicy)
	●	1 cooked beet (vacuum-packed is fine)
	●	¾ cup water (or as needed)
	●	2 tbsp soy sauce
	●	2 tbsp refined coconut oil (odorless and flavorless; you can substitute another oil, but the texture and richness may change slightly)
	●	1 heaping tsp miso paste (optional)
	●	1 tbsp olive oil
	●	½ tsp liquid smoke (optional, not essential at all)

Optional add-in: Finely chop 1 onion and 1 portobello mushroom, sauté until softened, and mix them into the batter.

## Instruccións

	1.	Mix: Pour the blended wet mixture into the bowl with the dry ingredients. Stir with a spoon until a dough forms.
	2.	Knead: Knead the dough by hand for 3–4 minutes. It should feel firm and slightly springy—that’s exactly what you want, as it means the gluten has developed.
	3.	Shape: Divide the dough into 10 equal portions and roll each into a sausage shape.
	4.	Wrap:
	●	Place each sausage on a small sheet of parchment paper and roll it up tightly, twisting the ends like a candy wrapper.
	●	Wrap each one again in aluminum foil and twist the ends firmly. Wrapping tightly helps the sausages hold their shape and stay dense while cooking.
	5.	Cook: Place the wrapped sausages into a pot of boiling water and cook for about 40 minutes.
	6.	Cool: Remove from the pot and let them cool completely before unwrapping. This helps them firm up.
